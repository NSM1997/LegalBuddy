# Legally - AI-Powered Legal Platform

Legally is an AI-powered legal platform aimed at revolutionizing legal practice by integrating advanced AI with comprehensive case management tools to streamline legal workflows, enhance decision-making, and improve access to justice.

- [Features](#features)
- [Installation](#installation)
- [Usage](#usage)
- [Technologies Used](#technologies-used)

## 🌟 Features

- **Documentation Management**: AI-assisted document drafting, version control, digital signatures, and legal citation checking.
- **Case Filing System**: Electronic filing interface for court submissions, jurisdiction-specific forms, and status monitoring.
- **AI Pre-Check & Triage**: Intelligent legal issue analysis, case merit assessment, and jurisdiction recommendations.
- **Case Management**: Comprehensive matter organization, task assignment, and integrated client communications.
- **Hearing Outcomes Portal**: Court decision repository with outcome analysis and appeal recommendations.
- **Judicial Dashboard**: Analytics for case load management, decision pattern analysis, and performance metrics.
